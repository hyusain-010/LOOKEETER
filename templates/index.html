About LOOKEETER

LOOKEETER is an OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) tool designed to analyze an email address using publicly available information. Enter an email address below to perform checks such as email validation, domain analysis, mail server (MX) lookup, and profile discovery features available within the platform.

Simply enter a valid email address and click Search. Depending on the information available, LOOKEETER may display domain details, mail server records, profile information, and links to perform additional public searches.

Warning: Information shown by LOOKEETER is collected from publicly available sources and automated lookups. Results may be incomplete, outdated, inaccurate, or belong to a different individual. Always verify information independently before making decisions based on the results.
